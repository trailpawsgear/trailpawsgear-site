A hiking harness has one job beyond comfort: giving you control without choking your dog when the trail gets narrow, steep, or crowded. We compared four of the most consistently recommended harnesses on the market — two from Ruffwear, two from Kurgo — looking at adjustment points, attachment options, and how each is actually built, not just what the marketing copy says.

Sizing is the single biggest source of returns on any harness, hiking or otherwise. Every brand below uses its own chest-girth measurement, not standard S/M/L — measure your dog before ordering, not after.

The short version

For most dogs and most trails, the Ruffwear Front Range is the harness we'd put on a first-time trail dog — comfortable, durable, and priced for everyday wear. Dogs tackling steep or technical terrain get real value from the Web Master's lift handle and extra adjustment points. Strong pullers do better in the Kurgo Tru-Fit, and if your dog is carrying its own water and snacks on multi-day trips, the Kurgo Baxter is built for that job specifically — not as a replacement for a daily harness.
