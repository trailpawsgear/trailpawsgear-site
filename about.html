About TrailPawsGear

TrailPawsGear exists because most "best dog gear" roundups are written by people who've never taken a dog past a city block — recycled spec sheets and stock photos, ranked by whoever pays the highest commission.

We do it differently: every guide starts from real specifications, cross-checked against verified owner reports and expert reviews, and we say plainly when a cheaper option is genuinely just as good as the expensive one. We're not embedded with any single brand, and no manufacturer gets to see or approve a review before it's published.

How we make money

TrailPawsGear is reader-supported. Some links on this site are affiliate links — if you buy a product after clicking one, we may earn a small commission from the retailer, at no extra cost to you. This never influences which products we recommend or how we rank them; our comparisons are written before we know which links will convert.

We're currently building out first-hand trail testing for every category. Where a guide is based on spec comparison and researched reviews rather than our own long-term testing, we say so directly in the article.

Who runs this site

TrailPawsGear is an independent publication. We keep the team behind it out of the byline by design — we'd rather you evaluate what we write on the strength of the research than the name attached to it. You can reach us directly at team.trailpawsgear@gmail.com with questions, corrections, or product suggestions.

Corrections

Specifications, pricing, and availability change. If you spot something out of date or incorrect, email us and we'll fix it — we'd rather be corrected than stay wrong.
