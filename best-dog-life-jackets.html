River crossings, lake swims, and paddling trips are where a dog's confidence in the water outpaces its actual swimming ability. A life jacket isn't just for weak swimmers — it adds visible flotation for spotting a dog from a boat, a rescue handle for pulling a tired dog out of current, and buoyancy that keeps a heavier or barrel-chested breed's head clear of the water when it tires. We compared four of the most consistently recommended dog PFDs (personal flotation devices) on buoyancy, handle design, and fit.

Unlike a harness, fit here isn't just about comfort — a loose life jacket rides up or twists in current, and a too-tight one restricts the paddling motion a dog needs to actually swim. Every jacket below uses girth (chest circumference) for sizing, not weight alone, so measure before you buy.

The short version

For most dogs that swim regularly, the Ruffwear Float Coat is the safest all-around bet — real buoyancy, a handle that doesn't get in the way, and full paddling freedom. If your dog is a large or heavy breed that needs serious flotation, the Non-stop Dogwear Protector's extra buoyancy and support are worth the higher price. Smaller dogs doing casual lake swims are well served by the Kurgo Surf N Turf, and if your dog only wears a life jacket a few times a season for boat trips, the Outward Hound Granby Splash covers the basics — visibility and a rescue handle — without the premium price tag.
