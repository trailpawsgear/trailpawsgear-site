A puppy's first swim is where a life jacket matters most. Young dogs haven't learned to control their breathing in water yet, they carry less natural buoyancy than a lean adult dog, and a startled puppy can panic-paddle itself into trouble in seconds. A properly fitted flotation vest turns that first lake or river trip into a confidence-building outing instead of a scare.

Puppy sizing is a different problem than adult sizing. Chest girth ranges run much smaller, weight minimums matter as much as maximums, and because a puppy keeps growing, the amount of adjustment range in the straps matters more than it does for a full-grown dog. We compared four vests that come in a genuine XX-Small or 2XS size, looking at girth range, how the foam and straps are arranged, and whether the handle on top is actually useful for lifting a wet, wriggling puppy out of the water.

The short version

Measure your puppy's chest girth before you order anything — it matters more here than for any adult-dog gear on this site, since a vest that's too loose can ride up and do the opposite of its job. For most puppies in the 11–16 lb range, the KONG Sport AquaPro gets the balance right between a secure three-buckle fit and a handle that holds up to a real lift. True toy breeds under 15 lbs do better in the EzyDog Micro DFD or, on a tighter budget, the ZippyPaws Adventure, which adjusts down to a 6-inch chest. If you'd rather buy one vest that keeps fitting as a medium-breed puppy grows, the Ruffwear Float Coat's wider strap range is worth the higher price.

Specifications and sizing are based on manufacturer listings and retailer data at time of writing and may change — always check the current size chart against your puppy's actual chest girth before ordering.
