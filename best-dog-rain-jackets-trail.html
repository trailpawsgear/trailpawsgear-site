A rain jacket and a winter coat solve two different problems. A winter coat traps body heat for genuinely cold days — we cover those in our guide to insulated winter coats. A rain jacket does one job only: keep a dog's coat dry through a wet hike without adding bulk or warmth, which matters on the mild-but-soggy days that make up most of fall and spring trail time, not just deep winter. We compared four jackets built specifically as rain shells, not insulated coats, looking at waterproof rating, coverage, and how each actually fastens on a moving dog.

Waterproof rating, measured in millimeters of water a fabric can resist before it seeps through, is the number that actually separates these jackets — not the price band. A jacket rated at 3,000mm will keep a dog dry through a light shower; one rated above 10,000mm holds up through sustained, heavy rain. Sizing still matters too: every brand below measures by back length or chest girth, not S/M/L, so measure your dog before ordering.

The short version

For a dog that's going to be out in real, sustained rain, the Non-stop Dogwear Fjord Raincoat 2.0 has the waterproof rating to back it up and is the one we'd reach for first. If your trail time is mostly everyday walks in light rain, the Ruffwear Sun Shower is lighter, more packable, and close enough on protection for most days. Tight on budget or hiking somewhere brushy and rough, the Kurgo Halifax is the sturdiest shell here for the price. And if your dog is between sizes or still filling out, the Hurtta Mudventure Eco's cinch system gives you room to adjust that the others don't.
